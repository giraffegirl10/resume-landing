### What I'm building

- A React frontend for browsing car parts and placing orders, connected to a FastAPI REST API.
- User accounts and login, carried over from the FastAPI Blog's authentication.
- Asynchronous SQLAlchemy queries and Alembic migrations for parts and order data.
- PyTest tests covering the API.
